PACE

Practice for Adaptive, Connected and Critically Engaged

The soft, collective, and public capabilities an exhibition builds rarely show up on a transcript. PACE records the traces they leave: you leave a trace by taking part, and deepen it by reflecting on what it built in you. Only deepened traces count. Reach the threshold in-school, and your archive becomes the evidence you write two of your Exhibits assignments from.

PACE records capability that a transcript cannot: participation leaves a trace, reflection deepens it, and only deepened traces feed assessment. The assistant is a cognitive prosthesis (Akata et al., 2020): it offers a question, never a score, so the judgement and the identity work stay with you (addressing the metacognitive-laziness risk, Fan et al., 2025). The in-school route to the threshold keeps the model equitable, the way Exhibits is the school's in-school alternative to the internship.
